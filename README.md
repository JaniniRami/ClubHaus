**Project** **Name**: ClubHaus | **Challenge** **Sector**: GJU Connect | **Status**: Prototype Ready

**1\. Idea overview**

ClubHaus helps students find the right events and the right teammates, without spam.

At GJU, this problem is amplified by the university’s unique structure and communication culture. Student life is fragmented across WhatsApp groups, informal broadcasts, and late official emails, creating message overload where relevant opportunities are easily missed. The physical separation between the Madaba and Jabal Amman campuses further weakens visibility and cross campus collaboration. Consequently, students remain trapped in 'academic silos,' socially isolated from peers in other faculties who share their interests. This structural disconnect stifles social innovation, leaving the immense collaborative potential of the GJU community largely untapped

---

**2\. Problem Statement**

Based on first-hand experience as a Club President and user interviews with student leaders, three core inefficiencies stifle campus life:

1. **The "Signal-to-Noise" Barrier:** Important announcements are buried under hundreds of irrelevant messages across multiple platforms or simply delivered by the university’s official emails just hours before the event starts, causing many interested students to never see event invitations, causing a drop in potential attendance.  
2. **The “Lonely Innovator” Paradox:** Innovation is blocked because talent is segregated by major. A Computer Engineering student with a startup idea struggles to find a Marketing student to team up with because their social circles do not overlap.  
3. **Administrative Blindness:** Most club leaders do not actively track registrations or attendance in a structured way. Without reliable data on who registered, who attended, or which events performed well, clubs are unable to measure impact, follow up with participants, or improve future activities.

These barriers matter because student organization participation is linked to measurable outcomes. In Ohio State's 2022 Student Life Survey, involved undergraduates reported higher belonging scores (2.96/4) than non-involved peers (2.77/4). ClubHaus addresses these structural barriers to help students access the clubs and events that support their success and sense of belonging. \-  Center for the Study of Student Life, "Student Organization Membership: Involvement and Belonging," The Ohio State University, September 2022.\]

---

**3\. Proposed Solution**

* **Smart Event Feed:** Centralizes all campus announcements into a single, AI relevance-based feed replacing scattered WhatsApp groups and late emails.  
* **Team Formation:** Quickly finds teammates with complementary skills using AI, which generates relevance-based matches to enable team formation in minutes rather than weeks.  
* **Talent Radar:** Replaces static student directories with a dynamic discovery feed, ranking students by compatibility based on complementary skills and shared interests.  
* **Seamless Event Management:** One-click registration with instant updates for any changes ensures students never miss events and reduces administrative burden.  
* **Engagement Tracking & Analytics:** Enables easy verification of event attendance through QR codes and tracks participation, providing clubs and the university with insights to understand engagement trends.

---

**4\. Expected Social Impact**

**Students**

* Break down social silos to connect students across majors, increasing their sense of belonging and community support through active student clubs participation.  
* Enable students to discover and attend relevant events more easily, with fewer notifications and an expected **15–20% increase** in verified event attendance.  
* Form teams quickly for competitions and projects.

**Clubs**

* Replace manual registrations and follow-ups.  
* Increase participation and engagement at events.  
* Access insights and feedback to improve future activities.

**University**

* Higher participation and competition success.  
* Gain actionable data on student engagement trends and active clubs.  
* Foster a connected, collaborative campus community.

---

**5\. Value Proposition**

* **Social Value Proposition**: ClubHaus strengthens campus communities by connecting students to relevant clubs, events, teammates, and opportunities based on shared interests and complementary skills.  
* **Impact Measure**: Social impact is measured through verified event attendance, team formation activity, and sustained engagement across clubs using QR-based tracking and participation data.  
* **Customer Value Proposition**: Students gain a personalized, noise-free experience, clubs increase engagement with less effort, and the university gains clear insight into student participation trends.

---

**6\. Beneficiaries**

* **Students:** Discover relevant events, find teammates, and build meaningful connections.  
* **Student Clubs:** Increase engagement and manage events easily.  
* **University:** Gain visibility into student participation and strengthen campus involvement.

* **Club Onboarding & Support:** Onboarding student clubs, supporting event setup, and collecting feedback.  
* **Pilot & Impact Monitoring:** Running pilots and tracking engagement and participation outcomes

---

**8\. Partners**

**Student Clubs:** Early adopters who create events, onboard members, and drive student engagement.

---

**9\. Resources**

* **Technology & Finance:** Cloud infrastructure for hosting with low operating costs.  
* **Access:** Permission to pilot with student clubs and students.

**10\. Sustainability Access** 

* ClubHaus uses containerized infrastructure to allow easy handover to **future student developers, open-source contributors, or GJU IT**. This empowers incoming students to maintain ownership and drive improvements, ensuring the platform remains a dynamic, long term asset beyond the founders' graduation.
